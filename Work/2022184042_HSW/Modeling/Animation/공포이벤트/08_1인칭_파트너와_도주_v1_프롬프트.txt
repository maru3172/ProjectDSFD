참조: 파트너 훼손0 정면 / 기존 마네킹 파도 컨셉
도주하면서 뒤를 돌아보는 시점으로 파트너와 추격하는 마네킹 파도를 함께 표현했다.

Use case: stylized-concept.
Create ONE landscape cinematic survival-horror game image, FIRST PERSON from the fleeing player's eyes, escaping TOGETHER WITH THE PARTNER from a huge surging wave of mannequins in a narrow abandoned department-store corridor.
Reference1 is PARTNER DESIGN ONLY: black twin braids, gray newsboy cap, dark cardigan with five white cat motifs over a closed white collared shirt, burgundy plaid pleated skirt, opaque pale tights, black ribbed legwarmers, black/white canvas sneakers. Preserve her recognizable anime-influenced face and all intact clothing; no tearing or exposure.
Reference2 is MANNEQUIN WAVE / ENVIRONMENT / MATERIAL reference: the bodies topple and tumble over each other under crowd pressure, not an ordinary orderly chasing crowd.

CAMERA AND ACTION MUST BE CLEAR: Player is RUNNING AWAY and has twisted their head to look BACK over their shoulder. Camera is at upright running eye height, not on the floor, not a third-person camera. We see the oncoming wave BEHIND US in the center/background. The PARTNER runs just beside and slightly behind the player in the near right third of the frame, coming toward the camera's retreating position, also fleeing AWAY from the wave. Her upper body leans into her sprint toward the near foreground; one leg drives forward, opposite arm pumps, twin braids stream backward toward the distant threat. She is looking toward the player with urgent frightened determination, not posing and not running toward the mannequins. She clasps the player's outstretched hand at the lower-right edge as they keep together. Only one first-person player's hand/forearm enters the foreground; NO player head, back, torso or separate full-body male character. Keep the partner clearly visible without blocking the wave. Natural running movement, intact skirt in place, no low-angle/upskirt framing.

WAVE: about 5–8 meters behind, dozens of rigid off-white display mannequins fill the corridor from wall to wall, surging forward toward the fleeing pair. Rear ranks shove front ranks down; more bodies climb involuntarily over bent backs and shoulders, building a dense curling crest nearly to the ceiling. Front mannequins pitch and slide forward at floor level; higher bodies topple horizontally into the front edge. Clearly show the wave's crest and the pressure-driven cascade, grasping plastic hands and colliding rigid limbs. Not water, not liquid, not floating mannequins or an explosion. A small strip of open corridor separates the pair from the approaching avalanche, showing imminent pursuit without anyone already caught.

Narrow corridor with shut storefronts, scuffed tile, overhead fluorescent fixtures and dark clothing displays. Pale fiberglass assembly seams and blank molded faces, artificial anatomically featureless mannequins, no people in the crowd. Dynamic wide-angle perspective, slight running camera tilt, selective motion blur at the corridor edges and braid tips, sharp readable partner and leading mannequin shapes, dust kicked up by the avalanche. Cold desaturated lighting with restrained warm emergency highlights. Realistic cinematic environment and materials blended with the established partner design. No blood, gore, wounds, sexualization, text, UI, logos, watermark, inset or collage. One continuous scene: fleeing together, a glance BACK at the pursuing mannequin wave.
